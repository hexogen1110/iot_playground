M5stickC Plus with ZephyrRTOS

Enable WiFi support in ZephyrRTOS

2024-12-26
#M5stickCPlus

The M5StickC Plus is powered by the ESP32-PICO-D4 module, which supports Bluetooth 4.2 and WiFi.

So, how do we enable WiFi functionality in Zephyr RTOS? Fortunately, Zephyr RTOS includes a variety of helpful examples. I referred to the zephyr/samples/net/wifi/apsta_mode example to enable WiFi on the M5StickC Plus.

Espressif Libraries

  1. Download Espressif Libraries
    ZephyrRTOS includes support for the ESP32 platform, but you need to confirm that the required Espressif HAL libraries are downloaded as part of your Zephyr installation. Use the following command in your Zephyr project directory to fetch the required dependencies:

  1. Check Espressif HAL Library
    After running west update, verify that the Espressif HAL libraries are located in the modules/hal/espressif directory of your Zephyr project.
